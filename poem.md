# Ode to `foo`

Before the real names come, there's `foo`,
a placeholder with nothing to prove.
It holds the place for what's still unknown,
and `bar` is never far behind.

It stands in for the function not yet written,
the variable still waiting for its role.
It asks for no meaning and no more than a name,
and it keeps the example simple.

So here's to `foo`, small and unassuming,
the first word of a thousand tutorials.
Every program starts with something like it,
and then we rename it.
